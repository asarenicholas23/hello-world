# EPA Visit Analytics Metric Definitions

Registration rate = registered visit rows / total visit rows.

Compliance/permit rate = rows with Permit Outcome of Permitted after visit, Permit renewed after visit, or Permitted (date unknown) / total visit rows.

Enforcement-visit share = rows where Visit Types includes Enforcement / total visit rows.

Visits per district/sector = count of cleaned visit rows grouped by District or Sector.

Time-to-registration = days between First Visit and the earliest permit issue/effective date when a registered facility's first dated permit is on or after the visit.

Weak compliance flags = sectors, districts, or facilities with high shares of Unregistered, No permit yet, or Permit renewal pending outcomes.
